Enjoy the journey

We're building a team where athletes of all abilities can enjoy the sport, take risks, build friendships, and be proud of their contribution.

Bring your best

We take responsibility for making the most of every opportunity through effort, consistency, and a willingness to challenge ourselves.

Pursue improvement

Focus on getting better, not just getting faster. We develop strong fundamentals, technical efficiency, racing, and progress at every level.

All for one, one for all

We are one team, regardless of ability or experience. We encourage one another, celebrate success, and create an environment where everyone belongs.

Our Focus

We believe every swimmer and diver can improve by focusing on the following areas

  • Technique

    Daily drill and application work to develop more efficient swimming and diving techniques.

  • Strength

    Increase power and explosiveness by building strength in the weight room.

  • Mindset

    Improve focus and the ability to tolerate being uncomfortable in pursuit of athletic excellence.

  • Teamwork

    Contribute to something bigger than yourself by supporting your teammates, school, and community.

  • Consistency

    Commit to showing up and working hard for yourself and your team every day.

  • Cardio

    Improve ablity to swim longer distances through daily, consistent aerobic training.
